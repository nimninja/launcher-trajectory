Shots

"Auto" sets each shot's v₀ so the arc passes through the goal height at distance x: v₀ = √( g·x² / (2·cos²θ·(y₀ + x·tan θ − y)) ). Drag a shot's slider to override it. Wheel RPM = 60·v₀ / (efficiency·π·D). x is measured to the center of the red HIVE's upward CELL opening: back distance is from the C4/C5 tile seam (the opening center sits a few inches behind it, toward the HIVE), side distance is from the HIVE's center line. The HIVE, frame and tiles follow the FTC BIOBUZZ competition manual (Figures 9-5, 9-8 to 9-11): pivot 43.95" up, 30° tilt, two 12.04"-deep hexagonal CELLS 18.84" apart with 20"×14" openings, up-CELL opening 53.5"–65.6" above the tiles (center ≈ 1.51 m). "Model" steps the ball (POLLEN 2.8" or NECTAR 3.6") along its arc: ✓ goes in = it crosses the opening with the whole ball inside the hexagon; ✗ hits wall = it touches a CELL wall, the other HIVE, or the frame first (red ✕ in 3D); • misses = neither. Window = how far the launch speed can be off (±) and still go in; the field-map heatmap shows it everywhere (red = tight or blocked, green = forgiving). Calibrate: for shots you've tested, enter the RPM you ran and the result; each one bounds the efficiency and the app picks the value that fits all of them. Click the floor in the 3D view or the field map to add a launcher; click a launcher dot in 3D to jump to its slider.
