Shots

"Auto" sets each shot's v₀ so the arc passes through the goal height at distance x: v₀ = √( g·x² / (2·cos²θ·(y₀ + x·tan θ − y)) ). Drag a shot's slider to override it. Wheel RPM = 60·v₀ / (efficiency·π·D). x is measured to the center of the red HIVE's upward CELL opening: back distance is from the C4/C5 tile seam (the opening center sits a few inches behind it, toward the HIVE), side distance is from the HIVE's center line. The HIVE, frame and tiles follow the FTC BIOBUZZ competition manual (Figures 9-5, 9-8 to 9-11): pivot 43.95" up, 30° tilt, two 12.04"-deep hexagonal CELLS 18.84" apart with 20"×14" openings, up-CELL opening 53.5"–65.6" above the tiles (center ≈ 1.51 m).
